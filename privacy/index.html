Privacy Policy

MotorHQ  ·  Last updated: October 2026

MotorHQ ("we", "our", or "the app") is a vehicle management app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information we handle, how we use it, and your rights.

Short version: your data stays on your device (and in your own iCloud account if you have sync enabled). We do not run our own servers, we do not collect analytics, and we do not advertise.

1. Information you enter

Everything you type or add into MotorHQ — vehicle registration numbers, service records, tyre details, glove-box documents, finance agreements, driving licence details, and maintenance notes — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Nothing is sent to us.

2. iCloud sync

If you are signed into iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled, your data is synced across your Apple devices via Apple's CloudKit service. This uses your own iCloud account; we have no access to it. Apple's iCloud privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

3. Government vehicle data

When you look up a registration plate, MotorHQ sends that registration number to UK government APIs to retrieve vehicle and MOT history:

  • DVSA MOT History API — operated by the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (UK government)
  • DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service — operated by the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency (UK government)
  • RDW Open Data (Netherlands users only) — operated by the Dutch vehicle authority

Only the registration number is sent. No personal information is included. The data returned (make, model, MOT results, tax status) is stored locally on your device.

4. Location

The Garage Finder and Fuel Finder features use your device's location to show nearby results via Apple MapKit. Your location is used only to perform the search in the moment you use it — it is not stored, logged, or transmitted to us.

5. Camera

The camera reg-plate scanner captures an image solely to read the registration number. The image is processed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. No image or image data is stored or transmitted anywhere.

6. AI diagnostic features

The Symptom Checker and Warning Light Advisor use Apple Intelligence, which runs entirely on your device using Apple's FoundationModels framework. Your diagnostic conversations are not sent to us or to any external server. Apple's terms apply to on-device AI processing.

7. In-app purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple's payment privacy policy applies.

8. Fuel prices

The Fuel Finder feature retrieves current fuel prices from a third-party fuel price data service. Your approximate location may be shared with this service to return nearby results. No personal identifying information is included in this request.

9. No analytics or advertising

MotorHQ does not include any advertising SDKs or analytics frameworks. We do not track how you use the app, and we do not share any information with advertisers or data brokers.

10. No account required

MotorHQ does not require you to create an account. We do not hold a database of users.

11. Children

MotorHQ is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Your rights (UK GDPR)

Because all personal data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise your rights directly:

  • Access / export — your data is in the app; you can view it at any time
  • Rectification — edit any record in the app
  • Erasure — delete individual records or use Settings → Remove All Vehicles to wipe everything
  • Portability — the Digital Service Book feature (Pro) exports your service history as a PDF

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at motorhq.app@gmail.com.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
